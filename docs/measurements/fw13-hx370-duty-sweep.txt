Read the table for two things:
  - STICTION: the duty where RPM first reads 0 on the way down.
    Any duty at or below that is a stopped fan, not a slow one.
  - CURVATURE: do not fit a line. The Intel board's curve was concave,
    and a linear fit put the firmware floor BELOW firmware.
